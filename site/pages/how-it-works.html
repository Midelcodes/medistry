Break-Glass in Four Steps

From Scan to Summary

  1. 1

    Scan

    A first responder scans the wallet card, wristband or NFC tag. The code opens an emergency gate that shows no personal data unless the patient chose to enable a minimal public badge.

  2. 2

    Sign in and state a reason

    A verified MDCN clinician signs in with a six-digit authenticator code and picks a clinical reason. Choosing “Other” requires written justification.

  3. 3

    Read the summary

    The clinician gets a read-only, sub-50 KB triage page for 60 minutes. The patient and their emergency contacts are alerted straight away, and the access is added to the audit chain.

  4. 4

    Co-sign for full history

    To see the multi-year history, a second, distinct attending clinician signs in on their own device and co-signs with a justification.
